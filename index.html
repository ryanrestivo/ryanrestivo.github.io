Hi, I'm Ryan Restivo

I've spent 17 years building tools that people actually use — from leading large-scale platforms at Newsday, to YESEO, a tool that has helped over 18,000 stories since launch using Large Language Models to help people make better editorial decisions. YESEO was a 2024 Online Journalism Award Finalist in the inaugural category of Excellence in AI Innovation.

My work has been featured by the Lenfest Institute, showcased at the JournalismAI Festival in London, and presented to the National Press Foundation to name a few. YESEO's development through the RJI Fellowship evolved into a tool that has impacted teams from all over the world. I've presented globally on how AI and automation transform workflows, from analyzing 60,000+ headlines to advising leadership on technology strategy.

Today, I lead product and emerging technology strategy at Newsday, overseeing iOS, Android, and 7+ TV platforms.

What connects it all? I build tools that remove friction from complex workflows. Technology should help teams reach better outcomes, not make decisions for them. Whether it's a cross-platform mobile ecosystem, an analytics tool, or an automated workflow system — the principle is the same: remove friction, create opportunity, and let the work get done better.
