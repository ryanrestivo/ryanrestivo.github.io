About Me

Observing problems isn't enough — I like finding solutions that serve others.

This philosophy has taken me through 17+ years of digital media, from building the Newsday mobile platform that tripled in-app subscribers to founding YESEO as a Reynolds Journalism Institute fellow, to being a 2026 AI Innovator-in-Residence at West Virginia University.

I led the full mobile platform modernization at Newsday (SwiftUI, Jetpack Compose) across iOS and Android. YESEO grew from an RJI Fellowship project to a 2024 Online Journalism Awards finalist.

Most recently, I partnered with The Oglethorpe Echo on the JournalismAI Innovation Challenge supported by the Google News Initiative on ways AI can help news-academic partnerships, such as The Oglethorpe Echo at the University of Georgia. YESEO has helped over 18,000 stories with SEO best practices.
