About Me

Who I Am

Observing problems isn't enough — I like finding solutions that serve others. This philosophy has taken me through 17+ years of digital media, from building the Newsday mobile platform that tripled in-app subscribers to founding YESEO (a free Slack app now serving 650+ newsrooms across 11 languages), to creating curriculum as AI Innovator-in-Residence at West Virginia University.

I led the full mobile platform modernization at Newsday (SwiftUI, Jetpack Compose) across iOS and Android, then drove an in-app subscriber growth surge referenced by Nieman Lab. YESEO grew from an RJI Fellowship project to an ONA Award finalist.

Most recently, I partnered with The Oglethorpe Echo on the JournalismAI Innovation Challenge (supported by the Google News Initiative). YESEO has helped over 18,000 stories with SEO best practices.
