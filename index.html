Who I Am

I'm a product leader and entrepreneur with 17+ years of experience building technology products that serve both audiences and organizations. My career spans mobile app development at Newsday, content coordination at MLB Advanced Media, and quality assurance at ESPN.

Since 2023, I've founded and built YESEO — a free Slack app that helps journalists improve SEO best practices.
