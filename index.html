About

Beyond the Headline

I've spent 17 years building things that people actually use — from the Newsday mobile app (SwiftUI, Jetpack Compose) that drove 265% YoY subscriber growth, to YESEO, the free AI tool that helps people improve their SEO in real time.

Before AI, I led mobile platform modernization at Newsday across iOS, Android, and TV apps. Before that, I worked at MLB Advanced Media, building video pipelines and metadata systems for 30 professional sports franchises. I've also contributed to the Blue Ribbon College Basketball Yearbook and served as a 2023-24 RJI Fellow.

Today, I'm an AI Innovator-in-Residence at West Virginia University, designing AI programming for the Reed School, delivering workshops, and advising on how technology can transform how teams work. I also continue to lead product and emerging technology efforts at Newsday.

What connects it all? I believe the best tools meet people where they already work. Whether that's Slack for journalists, a mobile app for readers, or an AI system for a newsroom — the goal is the same: remove friction and create opportunity.
